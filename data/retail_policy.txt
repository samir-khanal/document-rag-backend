Retail Store Customer Policy

Thank you for shopping with us. This document explains our policies for returns, exchanges, refunds, damaged products, and several other customer-service matters. Customers should keep their receipt or another valid proof of purchase because it may be required when requesting a return, exchange, or refund.

Returns and Exchanges

Most eligible products can be returned or exchanged within 30 days of the purchase date. The item should generally be unused and in its original condition. Customers should provide the original receipt or other valid proof of purchase. Some products may be excluded from the return and exchange policy, so eligibility can depend on the product category and its condition.

An exchange can be requested within the same 30-day period. Exchanges are subject to product availability. If the requested replacement product is unavailable, the customer may need to consider another available product or request a refund where the policy allows it.

Damaged Products

If a product arrives damaged, the customer should report the problem within 7 days of receiving the product. The issue may need to be verified before the store approves a replacement or refund. Reporting a damaged product after the seven-day period may affect eligibility.

Refunds

When a refund is approved, it is normally issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. The time required for the money to appear in the customer's account can vary depending on the payment provider. Approval of a refund does not necessarily mean that the money will appear immediately.

Products That May Not Qualify

A product may not qualify for a return or exchange if it has been used, if it was damaged by the customer after delivery, or if the customer cannot provide valid proof of purchase. Certain product categories may also have additional restrictions.

Store Information

Customers can contact the store's customer-service team for questions about an individual purchase. Store staff may request the transaction number, purchase date, product information, and proof of purchase when reviewing a request.

Shipping

This document does not specify whether standard shipping is free, paid, or dependent on the order amount. Customers should check the applicable shipping information for their order.

Payment Information

The store accepts several common payment methods. Specific payment methods and payment-provider processing times may vary by location and transaction type.
